# What "top 600" means (difficulty target)

Source: AREDL list data from github.com/All-Rated-Extreme-Demon-List/AREDL (`data/_list.json` plus per-level files).
That repo's snapshot is from **May 2025** and the live list has moved on since (new levels push old ones down). Treat
the positions below as a difficulty band, not exact current ranks.

common but less dominant than at the top, and precision/frame-perfect style is rarer.
